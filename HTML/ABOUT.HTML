About CarDekho

Your trusted destination for discovering, comparing, and choosing the perfect car.

Who We Are

CarDekho Clone is a pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript project built to replicate the structure and design of the original CarDekho platform. Our goal is to offer an interactive and responsive web experience where users can explore, compare, and learn about different car models effortlessly.

This project demonstrates the power of front-end design principles — from clean UI layouts to responsive grid systems — all crafted with simplicity and precision. It highlights how web design can deliver a real-world experience even without backend systems.

Our Mission

To make car discovery and comparison simple, engaging, and visually appealing. Our mission is to replicate CarDekho's essence through clean design, user-friendly navigation, and smooth interactions powered entirely by front-end technologies.
